# Content and image notes

`00:13` is an editorial index. It does not host or distribute films, episodes, books, comics, games, or playback files.

## Work information

Titles, release years, ratings, and short editorial notes are used for identification and recommendation. Detail links lead to Douban or Bangumi searches; scores are reference snapshots, not live rankings.

## Cover delivery

The repository does not copy third-party cover art into `assets/`. `data/covers.js` is a generated manifest of remote thumbnail URLs and provider labels. It is built from publicly accessible metadata providers including Wikimedia, TVmaze, Open Library, AniList, and an explicit source URL when needed.

Those images remain the property of their respective owners and providers. A future locally stored cover must have a documented, reusable licence. If an external image is unavailable, the interface falls back to the project’s own archive artwork.
